# Munirix by Adriel's Systems — DESIGN.md

## Visual World
Sculpted dark-steel precision derived directly from the Munirix emblem: deep obsidian void (`#07090E`), hairline steel dividers (`rgba(154, 176, 201, 0.14)`), brushed silver-steel secondary typography (`#9AB0C9`), frost white headlines (`#F2F6FC`), and a restrained dual-pole accent system drawn exclusively from the logo's crystalline spines — Runic Indigo (`#7B6BF2`) and Frost Cyan (`#38C6EC`).

## Guardrails (Impeccable Craft Floor)
- **No AI clichés or synthetic KPI badges:** Never display fabricated latency pills, model-name badges, or rainbow-colored icon tiles.
- **No nested cards:** Use one architectural surface divided cleanly by `1px` steel hairlines.
- **No eyebrows/kickers above headings:** Let every heading carry its own hierarchy and weight.
- **Uniform monochrome iconography:** Use `1.5px` stroke icons in Munirix steel (`#9AB0C9`), shifting smoothly to Frost Cyan (`#38C6EC`) on interaction.
- **Authored motion:** Preserve the horizontal `SoftAurora` beam tuned strictly to `#7B6BF2` → `#38C6EC`; apply Emil Kowalski's `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), explicit property transitions, and tactile `scale(0.97)` press feedback.
